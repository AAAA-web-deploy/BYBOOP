# Reference-site benchmarking — reviewed 2026-10-11

## WOJAK CTO — https://www.wojakcto.com/
**Observed:** oversized character repetition and "The Many Faces of Wojak" catalog; clear social/contract links; meme-generator navigation; simple ETH-oriented purchase guide.
**Borrow for BYBOOP:** build around the character's multiple emotions, make downloadable meme outputs an actual site feature, keep verified token info discoverable. **Do not copy:** Wojak character art, site's exact section arrangement, slogans, third-party logos, contract, or claims.

## PEPE — https://www.pepe.vip/
**Observed:** immediate oversized character-led hero; compact intro; image-led about and how-to-buy sections; obvious nav and actions; simple tokenomics and humorous disclaimer.
**Borrow for BYBOOP:** few words and big art, strong first-screen impression, four understandable steps for Ethereum newcomers. **Do not copy:** Pepe art, fonts, color palette by rote, tokenomics, or unsupported claims.

## FROGE — https://froge.vip/
**Observed:** bold editorial type, prominent character visual, scrolling copy stripe, numbered sections, game/leaderboard and ecosystem modules, copyable contract, clear unofficial/independent disclaimer.
**Borrow for BYBOOP:** large editorial typographic section headings, numbered comic chapters, looping ticker, purposeful native-JS interaction, measured motion. **Do not copy:** game concept, swap widget, brand copy, or sponsor claims.

## APU — https://apu.io/
**Observed:** emotion-oriented story, large image collections, community participation and meme culture as primary site content, real project information/risks, extensive downstream community features.
**Borrow for BYBOOP:** affection for the mascot first, art library, user-generated sharing, readable narrative. **Do not copy:** character, mascots, ranks, partnerships, claims, or tokenomics.

## The resulting original BYBOOP signature
"A scrappy crypto-cat field notebook": warm off-white paper, hand-inked charcoal outlines, cobalt propeller cap, silly Ethereum necklace, blue/red/yellow accents, imperfect rotated comic prints, hand-labeled mood stickers, a 3-beat picture-heavy origin story, and a real client-side meme generator. It should feel like a small piece of internet culture, not a SaaS or stock trading dashboard.

## Research notes and boundaries
Site contents were accessed via live page retrieval on Oct. 11, 2026; visible visual effects inferred from structure and media cues where direct frame-by-frame browser inspection was unavailable. Do not claim pixel-perfect recreation. This kit copies neither reference site images nor exact site layouts. All artwork is generated for BYBOOP or derived from previously approved BYBOOP character assets.
